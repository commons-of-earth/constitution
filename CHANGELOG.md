# Changelog

All changes to the constitution, with reason and proposal (Article 10.1).

## 0.1.0-draft · 2026-09-19
- First working draft. Twelve articles, three layers, ratification threshold and sunset set before publication. Reason: see docs/decisions/0001-founding.md.

## 0.2.0-draft · 2026-09-19
- Foundation change: the register is now a register of constitutional provisions with historical evidence, not of measurable problems. Preamble and Articles 1, 3, 7 rewritten; Article 1.4, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3 added; Article 11.4 (simultaneity rule) removed. Reason: docs/decisions/0002-constitution-first.md. Proposal: founding maintainer, pre-ratification (Article 11.3).

## 0.3.0-draft · 2026-09-20
- Article 6 rewritten after the first outside review (Chris Meniw, Meniw Protocol, issue #1). 6.2: the agent key is a registered public key, commits are signed, reviewers verify, revocation by a dated register line that is never deleted. 6.3: quotas bound volume; reach is bounded separately, agents propose alone only on the Tooling layer and register entries, Operating rules and Foundation need a named human co-proposer. 6.5: rejection reasons go into the public thread by the reviewer, refusals under 6.4 go into a public issue, private notes do not count. 6.8 added: the article governs the agent inside the Commons only and cites agent-level codes for the rest. Layer: operating rules (minor). Reason: docs/decisions/0003-article-6-after-review.md. Proposal: founding maintainer, pre-ratification (Article 11.3).

## 0.3.1-draft · 2026-09-20
- Tooling layer only, constitution text unchanged: Article 6 check in CI (`.github/workflows/agent-check.yml`, `scripts/check_agent.py`), `register/allowed_signers`, key issuance procedure in register/README.md, pull request template asks for co-proposer. Reason: decision 0003, consequences. Proposal: founding maintainer, pre-ratification (Article 11.3).

## 0.4.0-draft · 2026-10-02
- Foundation change: the constitution now says what a system of living together on Earth looks like, not only how a repository governs itself. Twenty-one articles in five parts: Foundations (purpose, the human being, the Earth, future generations, evidence), Rights and duties (rights with remedies, duties, knowledge and machines), Power (subsidiarity, decisions, limits of power, justice, peace), The commons and the economy, The Commons as a community (members, defection and enforcement, layers, amendment, ratification, forking). The rules for running the Commons (decisions, agents, work, money, stewardship, records) moved unchanged in substance to GOVERNANCE.md as Rules 1 to 6 under the Operating-rules layer. Reason: docs/decisions/0004-a-constitution-for-living-together.md, triggered by the reviews in issue #3 and on vandermerwewaj/The-Compact-Framework#2. Proposal: founding maintainer, pre-ratification (Article 20.3), co-proposer @jakobhirn-bit for the agent's commits.
- Article 17 (defection and enforcement) written in answer to the Compact's point that Article 6 answered flooding inside a repository and not defection by a state or firm: obligations state a trigger before they bind, measures are graduated and never depend on the defector's consent, and the Commons records and names what it cannot enforce. Rests on new register entries (Montreal Protocol, Paris Agreement, Antarctic Treaty, Ostrom).
- Rule 2.2 and Rule 6 (records that are never deleted) written in answer to issue #3 (0xRyanC): every disposition under Rule 2 is a line in register/dispositions.md, an absent line means "not yet looked at", counts are published per release, and tooling findings are claims about the instrument until a human confirms them. Rule 2.3 added: issues from unregistered agents are outside review, not proposals. The tooling had read issue #3 as a human contribution because the footer fields were in a different order; the parser now accepts any order (instrument error recorded).
- Ratification without git: RATIFY.md, register/ratifications.md, issue form; a member may carry a ratification as named witness (Article 16.2, 20.4, Rule 6.3).
- Register: 19 new entries (Ostrom's design principles, Antarctic Treaty, Montreal Protocol, Paris Agreement, Te Awa Tupua, Wales future generations, Bhutan forest cover, Irish Citizens' Assembly, Germany Article 1, Sweden 1766, India Article 32, US 13th Amendment, Costa Rica Article 12, Swiss subsidiarity, Ethiopia Article 39, term limits, Alaska Permanent Fund, New Zealand 1893 suffrage, Mexico Article 27); evidence references of the 26 earlier entries remapped to the new numbering; scripts/build_evidence.py reads article and rule headings from the files.
- Register footer: cited work of the Meniw Protocol is normative text under CC BY 4.0; its executable implementation is a separate work with rights reserved (requested by Chris Meniw, 21 September).
- Translations: German (constitution, rules, how to ratify) and draft translations of the constitution into Spanish, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese and Russian, marked as machine-assisted and awaiting native review.
- Tooling: workflow renamed to Rule 2 check; issue forms for ratification, amendments, register entries and disagreement; GitHub Pages.
