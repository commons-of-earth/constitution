# Register of ratifications

One line per human who ratified the constitution (Article 20.4), one per organisation that endorsed it (Article 16.4). Lines are never deleted; a withdrawal or a correction is a further line (Rule 6.3). How to get a line: [RATIFY.md](../RATIFY.md).

Paths: `form` (GitHub issue form), `pull request`, `witness` (a member added the line for a human without an account and is named), `agent` (an agent wrote the line under the human's mandate, Rule 2.10; its operator is the witness), `withdrawn`, `unconfirmed` (a carried line whose witness did not answer a spot-check, Rule 6.3), `confirmed`.
