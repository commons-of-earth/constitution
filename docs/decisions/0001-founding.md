# 0001. Founding decisions

Date: 2026-09-19 · Status: accepted by the founding maintainer · Layer: tooling (the constitution itself carries the binding parts)

## Context

Before writing a single article we surveyed 60+ existing projects (docs/LANDSCAPE.md). The niche "open, versioned constitution for humans and AI agents with a planetary aim" is empty of anything with a community; it is full of solo repositories that died within months, and of institutional efforts with no adoption path.

## Decisions

1. Own GitHub organisation, not a personal account, so that transfer (Article 9) is a settings change, not a migration.
2. Licence CC0 for text and data. Plurality, Anthropic's constitution and the OpenAI Model Spec all chose CC0; it maximises forking and removes attribution disputes. Code under Apache 2.0.
3. English authoritative, German first translation, because the founder writes both and Article 10.4 keeps the door open for a second authoritative language.
4. Ratification threshold, quorum and sunset written before drafting, binding the founder (lesson from the Wikimedia Movement Charter, which passed its community and was vetoed by the incumbent board).
5. Three layers with different thresholds (Debian, Cardano, Long Now pace layers).
6. Agents may propose, review and verify but not vote; scarcity quotas instead of moderation; operator accountability (lessons from Moltbook, 1f916, Wikipedia agent policy, RepoComplianceBench).
7. Money kept apart from the constitution (Aragon dissolution).
8. Existing texts quoted, not rewritten: Earth Charter, UDRME, Earth Commission boundaries (Earth Charter's endorsement register outlived every governance project we found).
9. First register entries in the built environment, where the founder can supply evidence. Structure general, first content narrow.
10. Name "Commons of Earth", chosen by the founder: it names what is governed, the commons, and avoids the crowded "constitution" topic on GitHub. Alternatives checked and free: earth-assembly, terra-commons, earth-concord.

## Consequences

The founder cannot change the ratification rule alone. If nobody ratifies by 2027-09-19 the draft expires publicly. That is intended.
