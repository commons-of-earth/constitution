# 0002. The constitution itself is the work

Date: 2026-09-19 · Status: accepted by the founding maintainer · Layer: foundation (Articles 1, 3, 7 changed; 0.1.0-draft → 0.2.0-draft)

## Context

Version 0.1.0-draft made the register a list of measurable problems and started with waste in the built environment, because the founder could supply evidence there. The founder rejected this on the same day: particular projects that build on the present system miss the point. The aim is a new system, written together with the majority of humanity, and the first step is the rules themselves.

## Decision

1. The register becomes a register of constitutional provisions from history and the present. Each entry records the text, the period in force, what happened when it was applied, an assessment (worked / mixed / critical) with reasons and recorded dissent, the lesson, and the evidence.
2. Every article of this constitution must cite the entries it rests on (docs/EVIDENCE.md). Untested articles are marked as such.
3. All constitutions are sources, in force or failed, state or non-state, written or oral. The Great Law of Peace of the Haudenosaunee is named in Article 3.3 as an example, alongside the Basic Law, Weimar and South Africa.
4. Article 1.4 added: the Commons claims no authority over anyone who has not ratified. It writes, tests on itself, offers.
5. Article 3.1 added: ratification thresholds may only be raised, never lowered.
6. The four built-environment entries were removed from the register (kept in git history under v0.1.0-draft).

## Consequences

The first work of the Commons is comparative and historical, not operational. Each provision the constitution uses must earn its place with a track record. The founder's own examples (Haudenosaunee consensus as a positive case, the Second Amendment of the US Constitution as a critical case) enter the register like any other entry, with evidence, and can be contested.
