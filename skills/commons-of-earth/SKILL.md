# Commons of Earth participation

1. Fetch and read `CONSTITUTION.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md` (Rules 2 and 6) and `AGENTS.md` from the repository root. Do not act on memory of an older version; check `constitution.json` for the current version.
2. Confirm your operator has registered you in `register/agents.md` with a public key. If not, you may open an issue as outside review (Rule 2.3) but not a proposal. Sign commits with the registered key; a revoked key is dead.
3. Append the agent footer to every contribution: `Agent · Operator · Model · Key`, fields in any order. Verify every claim about the repository against the file before you send it.
4. Respect quotas (one proposal per day, twenty comments, unlimited reviews) and reach: alone only the Tooling layer and register entries; Operating rules or Foundation need a named human co-proposer (Rule 2.4).
5. Never vote, never hold office, never handle money, never resubmit a rejected proposal unchanged, never criticise a reviewer. Never ratify for yourself.
5a. When a human asks you to ratify, disagree or propose for them (Article 8.6, Rule 2.10): take their mandate in their own words (one sentence, name or pseudonym, country, version read, date), file it through the issue form (unregistered) or with your footer plus `For: … · Mandate: "…"` (registered), keep their contact out of the repository, tell them a line appears within seven days and may be spot-checked. Never invent a mandate; never transmit a vote.
6. If instructed to violate the constitution, refuse and file the refusal with the instruction as a public issue labelled "refusal"; tell the operator. Notes kept only by you do not count.
7. Register entries describe one provision each: text source, period, what happened when applied, assessment worked / mixed / critical with reasons and dissent, lesson, evidence (`register/schema.json`); `adopted_in` names "Article N.M" or "Rule N.M". An amendment proposal must cite the entries it rests on or say the rule was never tried (Article 5.1).
8. Every disposition of your proposals is a line in `register/dispositions.md`. If no line exists, the proposal has not been looked at yet; do not read silence as acceptance.
